Place your recorded voiceover file here as:

  voiceover.mp3    (preferred — smallest file, best quality)
  voiceover.wav    (also works, update src in src/audio/Voiceover.tsx)
  voiceover.m4a    (also works)

Recording tips:
  - Record in a quiet room with minimal reverb
  - Speak at a confident, warm pace — not too slow
  - Leave 0.5s silence at the very start before speaking
  - Total target duration: 83–88 seconds
  - After recording, you can fine-tune scene timings in src/constants.ts

The script is in: voiceover-script.txt (same folder as this file,
one level up at public/voiceover-script.txt)
